Apex GP

Powered & organised by Jumay™ Corporation

Time attack. Pick a car, set a lap time, beat your record. Stay inside the white line: a lap with all four wheels off the track is deleted.

Realism & assists

Only assists that exist in this game are listed. ABS and traction control matter: with them off, braking hard while turning loses front grip, and full throttle in low-speed corners makes the rear step out. Gearbox is always automatic.

Camera & field of view

Real-driver FOV: horizontal FOV = 2 × atan(screen width ÷ (2 × eye distance)). Example: a 32" monitor (71 cm wide) viewed from 80 cm gives about 48°. Sit closer or use a wider screen and the angle grows, so the view matches what you would see through a real windscreen. Applies to the cockpit camera (C).

Multiplayer offline

    Ghost racing over the 7MIT realtime server: everyone in the same room and on the same circuit sees each other on track and in the timing tower, with no collisions. Share a room code with friends, or use PUBLIK. Only your name, car position and lap times are sent, nothing is stored.

    Race engineer guide
    • Tyres: watch the % next to the compound. Grip falls the more you corner hard and brake, so smooth inputs make a stint last. Soft is fastest but wears about 3x faster than Hard.
    • ERS: hold ERS (Q, or the on-screen button) for extra power on exit and down straights. It drains in about 12 s and refills when you brake or lift. Save it for DRS straights and corner exits.
    • DRS: open it (E / Shift) in the green-marked zones with the throttle fully down. Brake or lift to close it.
    • Braking: follow the racing line colours: red = brake, yellow = lift, green = full throttle. Without ABS, release the brake a little as you turn in.
    • Track limits: a lap with all four wheels off the track is deleted, so use the full width but not more.
    • Calibrating pace: use Medium tyres, set a reference lap with assists on, then switch to the Realistic preset and see how many tenths each assist was worth.

    Fuel mixture, safety cars, penalties, AI opponents and race distance are not part of this time-attack game.
